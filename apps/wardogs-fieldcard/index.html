Get paid,
stay in the zone.The whole game in one sentence

100 players, three teams, one Control Zone. Every 30 seconds the team with the most players inside scores a point; first to 100 wins. You buy your kit each life with cash you keep between matches — so every death costs real money, and every revive, lift, delivery or spot earns it back.

Your first three lives

  1. Take the free starter kit, not a $4,000 rifle. You can go negative. A cheap life you survive beats a rich one you lose in ninety seconds. Rebuy your last loadout with one button when you die.
  2. Spend five minutes in the Firing Range. Bind lean, sprint and hold-breath somewhere comfortable, set helicopter mouse control, then leave. (See Settings.)
  3. Find a ride at spawn. There's no squad spawning and the map is enormous. Hop in anyone's truck or Littlebird heading to the zone — tip the driver with F1 when you arrive.
  4. Stand in the Control Zone. That's the whole objective. Occupying pays cash on its own. If the Hot Zone is safe, stand in that instead: you count as two players and earn double.
  5. Carry bandages and use them on other people. Healing and reviving pay reliably, don't need unlocks, and level your Medic track toward the defibrillator (Medic level 9).
  6. Ping everything. Middle mouse. Spotting enemies pays and levels Recon. Voice: J squad, K proximity.

Session checklist

Rule of thumb from the beta: if you can't afford to lose it twice, don't buy it. Tanks, top-tier rifles and fast healing items are where new players bleed money. Vehicles and heavy kit are for once you've banked a cushion.

How a match actually flows

Spawn & buy

Vendor tabs: Weapons, Equipment (incl. Building), Logistics, Vehicles. Slot 1 primary, 2 sidearm, 3 specialist item; 4–0 quick slots from your tac-vest.

Travel

Drivers and pilots get paid per passenger they drop in the zone. Littlebirds are the fast taxi; trucks carry pallets for FOBs. Parachute: Space deploy, C cut.

Fight for ticks

Bodies in the zone every 30 s. The Hot Zone drifts slowly inside it; whoever holds it is usually winning the tick. Expect tanks, helis, mortars and medics chain-reviving.

Pull the Hot Zone

2–5 drill towers each hide one digit of a code. Enter the full code to drag the Hot Zone to a tower. Or build a fuelled Drill Rig at your FOB and drag it to you.

Bank it

Profit carries over. Losses do too. Periodic wipes reset economy and progression, so nobody stays untouchably rich.

Level tracks

Six progression tracks (Assault, Medic, Recon, Support, Driver, Pilot) level from what you do. Levelling unlocks the right to buy gear — nothing is free.

Roles are what you carry, not what you picked

There is no class screen. Six progression tracks level from your actions, and any loadout can mix tools from all of them — a sniper can carry a defib, a driver can run an LMG. Pick a job for this life, buy for that job, and don't buy for jobs you're not doing.

Squad shape that worked in the beta: one medic, one driver or pilot who keeps a return route open, one logistics/builder, and the rest infantry holding the zone. A squad farming cash far from the objective can be rich and still lose.

Cash is the scoreboard that follows you home

Hot Zone maths

Red has 20 in the Control Zone, you have 17. Normally Red takes the tick. But four of your 17 are in the Hot Zone, so they count as 8 — your total is 21 and you score. That's why it's the bloodiest 50 metres on the map.

Tower codes

Each tower holds one digit. Share them in team chat with position — post XX5 if you learned the third digit is 5 — so the team can fill in the blanks. Entering the full code pulls the Hot Zone to that tower. Tower-hopping by Littlebird is a legitimate full-match job.

Drill Rig

Build one at a FOB, keep it fuelled, and the Hot Zone comes to you. Then everyone comes to you too, so fortify first: anti-air, HESCO, mortars, a repair station, a vehicle parked behind the wall for spawns.

What costs

  • Every life. You rebuy your kit when you die. Expensive kit → expensive death.
  • Vehicles. A lost tank or heli is the single biggest way to go negative.
  • Consumables. Faster healing items, weapon mods, extra mags all add up.
  • Weight. Not cash, but heavy kit slows you and worsens sway — see Loadout.

Safe income loop for a new player

  1. Cheap kit + bandages.
  2. Ride to the zone. Tip the driver.
  3. Stand in the Hot Zone when it's yours, the Control Zone when it isn't.
  4. Ping, heal, revive. Loot better guns off the dead if you're broke.
  5. Bank a cushion, then buy the toy.

Backpack = how much you carry (grid inventory — a 2×1 magazine eats two squares). Tac-vest = how fast you reach it. A special rig lets you carry two primaries.

Heavier kits also add aim sway. Logistics drivers who barely shoot shouldn't wear the heaviest armour — it just raises the cost of the death.

Buy for the job

Going into the zone

Rifle, sidearm, armour, ammo, a grenade or two, bandages. Add a defib if you have it — reviving in the Hot Zone is double pay.

Running towers

Light kit, SMG or carbine, a rangefinder, something to clear a room. Speed matters more than plates.

Driving supplies

Cheap gun, minimal armour, wrench. Spend the money on the truck and pallets, not on a kit you'll lose to a mine.

Flying

Almost nothing on your body. The helicopter is the loadout. Bring flares (V) and know where the AA is.

Anti-vehicle

Launcher in slot 3, spare rockets in the backpack. A packed heli or tank kill is a huge payout — position near vehicle lanes into the zone.

Broke

Free starter kit. Loot dead players' weapons. Ping, heal, stand in the zone. You'll be positive again within a match.

FOBs: a crate you buy, place, and keep feeding

A Forward Operating Base defines a build radius (blue square), stores four supply types, and lets teammates build inside it. It is not a spawn flag — but a vehicle parked behind its walls is, effectively. A fresh FOB in the beta started with roughly 1,250 Supply / 100 Fuel / 100 Ammo / 100 Parts, plus a small and medium hammer, wrenches, signal grenades and bandages.

Build one

  1. Buy the FOB from the vendor (Equipment › Building), plus the hammer tier you need.
  2. Buy supplies — Ammo, Building, Fuel, Mechanical — and pallets from the Logistics tab. A truck or heli carries them; your backpack can't.
  3. Place the FOB first, then bring pallets inside the blue build radius. Deploy pallets while moving with G.
  4. Unload supplies into the FOB by hand. A crate sitting next to it does nothing. Interact with F.
  5. Build: hold the hammer, place, rotate R, snap toggle B, dismantle V. Strike each yellow X with the matching hammer.
  6. Keep it fed. Nothing restocks itself. Send the next pallet for whatever you're about to spend.

Four supplies, four jobs

SupplyFeeds

Hammers gate the menu

  • Small — sandbags, barriers, barbed wire, hedgehogs, recon tents (these few also work without a FOB).
  • Medium — the above plus HESCO and larger structures.
  • Large (tier 3) — anti-air, mortars, kamikaze drones, Drill Rig.
An enemy FOB dies to one IED. If you're building, hide it behind terrain and defend it; if you're not, a cheap explosive on an unguarded enemy FOB is one of the best trades in the game.

Vehicles seen in the beta

Littlebird

Light helicopter. The zone taxi and tower-hopper. Cheap enough to be everywhere, fragile enough to die to a rifle. Pilot track.

Transport heli

Bigger, carries a squad and supply cases. Full of passengers it is also the single richest kill on the map — fly around the AA.

Ural / cargo trucks

Pallet haulers. The logistics backbone: one Ural load can stand up a basic FOB. Driver track.

Pickup / buggy

Fast, cheap, unarmoured. Best cash-per-passenger ratio for a new driver.

MRAP / APC

Armoured transport. Survives mines and small arms; a mobile spawn behind a FOB wall.

Tank

Expensive, decisive, and a magnet for every launcher on three teams. Not a first-week purchase.

If a keybind keeps disappearing

A known playtest issue. Check Settings › Controls › Keybindings for Move Forward/Back, Sprint, Focus, Jump, Deploy Parachute and Primary Fire. If it recurs, fully close the game and relaunch.

Performance targets (developer)

RTX 3070 / RX 6700 XT-class GPUs were quoted for 4K Medium at 60+ FPS upscaled. Visibility settings matter more than pretty ones on a 2×2 km battlefield.
