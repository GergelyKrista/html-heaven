Comprehensive Strategy: Facebook

01 The Resharing Mandate
Viral growth on Facebook is impossible if your audience cannot resharing your work. Ensure your post audience is set to Public. Private or "Friends-only" posts hide the Share button from potential new fans.
Privacy Check

Go to Settings > Audience and Visibility > Posts. Set "Who can see your future posts?" to Public. This is the single biggest factor in organic distribution.

02 Binary Engagement Loops
Algorithm priority is awarded to Velocity of Interaction. A/B polls or binary questions (Yes/No) create high-volume, low-friction comment waves that propel your post into the Newsfeed.
The A/B Script
"Planning next week's guide for [Topic]. A) Focus on Automation Systems B) Focus on Content Strategy Which one is your priority? Drop A or B below — I'll send the draft to the first 50 people who vote."
03 The Ghost Link Protocol
Facebook severely throttles posts containing external URLs. To maintain reach, publish your post as Image + Text with zero links. Wait 15 minutes, then edit the link into the first comment.
04 4:5 Visual Dominance
Square (1:1) is dead. 4:5 Portrait images occupy 20% more vertical screen space on mobile. In 2026, this extra real estate is the difference between a "scroll-by" and a "stop-scroll."
05 Sentiment-Driven Captions
The algorithm now tracks Comment Sentiment. Posts that trigger positive, long-form discussion (over 4 words per comment) are distributed to "Friends of Friends" automatically.
06 Native Video Hooks (3s Rule)
Don't link to YouTube. Upload natively. Your first 3 seconds must be a "Visual Hook" (text overlay or motion) to trigger the 'Watch' feed distribution.
07 Group Sentiment Mirroring
Distribution is now Group-centric. Mirror the language and specific jargon of a Group when sharing your Page posts to ensure higher Member Appreciation signals.
08 Direct-to-DM "Nudge"
End your posts with "DM me the word [KEYWORD] for [RESOURCE]." This signals deep intimacy to the algorithm, ensuring that user sees your main feed for the next 7 days.
09 The "Context-Value-Action" Framework
Post structure: Context (Why now?) → Value (3 bullets of insight) → Action (Simple CTA). This scannability is optimized for the mobile user's attention span.
10 Community Polls for Metadata
Native FB Polls aren't just for engagement; they tag your profile with interest metadata. Use them weekly to refine how the AI categorizes your audience.

Comprehensive Strategy: Instagram

01 Save-Optimized Architecture
In 2026, a "Save" is worth 10x more than a "Like." Structure carousels as Modular Toolkits. The final slide should be a high-contrast checklist readers will want to reference later.
Carousel Formula
Slide 1: Identify a painful industry myth. Slides 2-8: Step-by-step fix with screenshots. Slide 9: The "Cheat Sheet" Summary. Slide 10: "Save this for your next [Project]."
02 Perfect Retention Reels
High watch-time triggers the Explore feed. End your Reel with a sentence that loops perfectly back to the first second of the video, encouraging multiple viewings per user.
03 Semantic Keyword SEO
Keywords in your caption and Alt-Text are now more powerful than hashtags. Manually edit 'Advanced Settings > Alt-Text' for every post using niche-specific keywords for semantic indexing.
Privacy & Security

Enable "Hidden Words" in Privacy settings to automatically filter bot comments and spam. This keeps your engagement signal clean and professional.

04 Story-to-DM conversion
DMs are the strongest intimacy signal. Use "Keyword Triggers" (e.g., "Reply with START"). Each reply creates a private thread, ensuring the algorithm shows them your next 3 feed posts.
05 The "Secondary Hook" Rule
Instagram re-serves carousels to non-interactors by showing the second slide. Ensure your Slide 2 is as visually arresting as Slide 1 to capture the second-look audience.
06 Story Sticker Density
Engagement signals on Stories are binary. Use Polls, Sliders, and Quizzes daily. Every tap on a sticker is a "Vote of Confidence" for your profile's authority.
07 Broadcast Channels for Intent
Move your high-intent fans into a Broadcast Channel. This bypasses the feed entirely, delivering notifications directly to their inbox—the highest-converting space on the app.
08 Collab Post Synergy
Co-authoring a post with a peer shares engagement signals across both audiences. It effectively doubles your footprint in the Explore feed for zero extra cost.
09 Hiding "Like" Counts
For accounts under 50k, hiding "Like" counts often increases comment volume. It shifts the user's focus from "social proof" to "personal engagement," humanizing the brand.
10 Data Usage Quality Check
Verify Settings > Data Usage > "Upload at Highest Quality" is ON. Instagram defaults to compression to save server space—don't let them kill your 4K production value.

Comprehensive Strategy: Substack

01 The Restack Multiplier
Substack Notes is the discovery engine. When Restacking your own post, add a Behind-the-Scenes reflection. Don't just promote; humanize the effort to stop the scroll.
The Notes Script
"I almost deleted today's draft because [Reason]. But then I realized [New Truth]. If you've ever felt [Specific Emotion], this one was written for you. Link to full piece below."
02 Mobile Pattern Interrupts
Readers scan; they don't read. Use a "Visual Reset" (bold quote, horizontal rule, or small 300px image) every 250 words to refresh focus and maintain scroll depth.
The 102KB Limit

If your email exceeds 102KB, Gmail clips the bottom. This kills your CTA. Keep images <150KB and text focused to ensure deliverability.

03 Recommendation Syndicates
Growth is fueled by other writers. Reach out to 3-5 creators for a Recommendation Exchange. This accounts for 60% of organic growth in the 2026 ecosystem.
04 Welcome Sequence Funnel
Your first automated email has a 90% open rate. Curate your "Best Of" list immediately to hook the reader into your highest-value work and ensure long-term retention.
05 The Paid Tier "Specific" Offer
Don't sell generic "support." Sell a specific Transformation (e.g., a private PDF guide or a monthly AMA link). Specificity converts 3x better than altruism.
06 "Open Thread" Rituals
Monthly Open Threads convert passive readers into active community members. High comment volume drives Notes discovery and algorithm favor.
07 Smart Cropping for Notes
Ensure your social preview image (1456x1048) centers the most critical element. Substack's AI cropping on mobile is aggressive; keep the "eye" in the center.
08 Shareable Quote Graphics
Generate a high-contrast 1200x630px image for your core thesis. These are "Restack Bait" on Notes, often going viral even if the full article is behind a paywall.
09 The TL;DR Header
Start every long-form essay with a 3-bullet "Executive Summary." This respects the reader's time and increases the conversion from 'Click' to 'Full Read.'
10 Cross-Pollination vs. Guest Posting
Guest posting on larger publications is a high-effort, high-reward growth lever. Aim for one external collaboration per quarter to tap into established trust.

The 2026 Core Directive

Owned media is the only sustainable asset. Use social for discovery, but optimize every touchpoint for direct conversion to your private network. Build on property you own, not property you lease.
