Tabletop Compass

A reference for picking your first tabletop RPG system

Your group is rotating GMs. Some of you have never run a game. This is a side-by-side look at the major RPG systems — what you need to play, how steep the climb is, and where to read more.

How to read each entry

New GM Friendliness 5 = great first-time pick. 1 = bring experience.
Rule Complexity How much you need to know before play. 5 = thick rulebook, many edge cases.
Character Freedom 5 = invent almost any concept. 1 = pick from defined options.
Homebrew Friendly 5 = systems designed to be modified, huge community content.
