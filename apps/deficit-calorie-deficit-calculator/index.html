Rules that do most of the work
  1. Hit protein first. If you hit the protein target, you can miss calories by 200 either way and still progress.
  2. Weigh daily, trust the weekly average. Day-to-day numbers are noise — especially the day after heavy lifting. The 7-day trend is the signal.
  3. Weekends count. Two "off" days can erase a weekday deficit. Log honestly or pick a looser target you can live with.
  4. Alcohol is the biggest silent lever. Four pints is ~800 kcal of nothing — not banned, just accounted for.
  5. If lifts die for 3–4 weeks straight, eat 200 more. Keeping muscle in a deficit is the whole point; strength is the proxy for how it's going.
