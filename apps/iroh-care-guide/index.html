🐩 Iroh's Care Guide

2-year-old Standard Poodle · 25kg

📅 Daily Routine 🚨 Emergency 🏥 Medical 🐕 Behavior 🌳 Outdoor 🏠 Indoor 🏞️ Parks

🍽️ Feeding Summary

  • Per meal: 3 sections of wet food + add water to the food
  • Meals per day: 2 (morning + afternoon/evening)
  • Hydration: If he hasn't drunk enough, give coconut water before last walk
  • Rule: ONLY feed AFTER walks, no play for 40+ mins after eating

🌅 Morning (7:30-8:30am)

  • Walk: 30-60 mins, off lead in park
  • Inhaler: 1 puff, 8-10 breaths
  • Breakfast: 3 sections of wet food + water added
  • Say "good morning" when you wake up - he likes this!
  • He can sleep in easily - no rush

🌞 Lunch (12:00-14:00)

  • Walk: 15-30 mins, on lead around block OR off lead in park

🌆 Afternoon (17:00-19:00)

  • Walk: 15-30 mins, on lead around block OR off lead in park
  • Dinner: 3 sections of wet food + water added

🌙 Evening (21:00-22:00)

  • Final toilet walk: 10 mins - school and back is enough
  • Inhaler: 1 puff, 8-10 breaths
  • Sleep in same room with you - he likes company
  • If leaving door open, close the baby gate

🫁 Asthma Care

  • Has doggy asthma - needs inhaler twice daily
  • Watch for coughing after running or getting too excited
  • If he starts coughing: stop activity and give him gentle pets to calm down
  • Let him rest and breathe normally before continuing

👁️ Eye Care

  • Eyes can get a bit gunky - this is normal for him
  • Clean gently with a wet towel when needed
  • Just wipe away any discharge around the eye area

💊 Daily Medical

  • Inhaler: 1 puff, 8-10 breaths, twice daily (morning & evening)
  • 25kg, 2 years old, male poodle

🚽 Toilet Habits

  • Frequency: 2-4 poos per day (completely normal)
  • Signals: Licks your elbow and stares when needs toilet
  • Generally good at holding it, but watch for his signals

🎾 Social & Play Behavior

  • Loves to play with anyone - very social and friendly
  • Never tells off other dogs - very gentle and non-aggressive
  • Gets excited around female dogs, especially those in heat
  • Good with other dogs off-lead but avoid on-lead greetings

🧸 Excitement & Attention-Seeking

  • Steals pillows when excited or wanting attention
  • Also steals clothing - keep clothes away as he'll swallow socks!
  • Gets very excited when you arrive home or first arrive
  • If over-excited: put him in hallway until he calms down
  • Use "Leave it" command when he's being too grabby

🍽️ CRITICAL: Eating & Exercise Rules

  • ONLY feed AFTER walks - never before exercise
  • NO play for 40+ minutes after eating - prevents bloat
  • He might initiate play but should be settled - redirect to calm activities
  • Let him rest quietly after meals - very important!

🍽️ Eating Habits

  • Not super excited about meals - this is normal for him
  • May walk away and come back to finish - don't worry!
  • Cheese trick: Add a tiny bit of cheese to encourage eating
  • Don't feed from the table - he'll become pushy

🗣️ Key Commands

  • "Leave it" - Most used! For anything, especially when over excited
  • "Drop it" - For chicken bones/street finds (he WILL drop it)
  • "Touch" - Extend arm down, he'll come boop your hand
  • "Sit" & "Down" - He knows these but will listen to you
  • "Search" - Use when scattering treats for calming
  • "Ready" - Emergency recall: say "ready", pull out tug toy, run in opposite direction

🔍 His Signals to You

  • Licks elbow + stares: Needs toilet OR wants cuddles
  • Barks at window/balcony: Say "thank you", close curtains if continues

💡 General Personality

  • Generally well-behaved but can be cheeky and pushy
  • Takes his time with commands - say them ONCE and be patient
  • Likes company and sleeping in the same room as you
  • Enjoys being greeted with "good morning" when you wake up
  • Can sleep in easily - no rush in the mornings

🚶‍♀️ Walking Basics

  • Use FRONT clip of harness
  • No on-lead dog greetings - short lead and treats to pass other dogs
  • Good recall off-lead, backup: say "ready" then pull out tug toy
  • AVOID using the ball

⚠️ Off-Lead Precautions

  • Keep away from dogs on lead - he licks female pee/dogs
  • No off-lead near roads - he chases squirrels intensely
  • He won't run away but gets very focused on squirrels
  • With ball: he'll return but hard to get ball from him

🌱 Walking Notes

  • Eats grass when thirsty - give him water
  • His water bottle is in the cupboard, or there's a collapsible water bowl in his bag

⚠️ Important Indoor Rules

  • Keep clothes away - he'll swallow socks!
  • Don't feed from the table
  • If over excited: put in hallway until he calms down

Daily Indoor Care

  • Scatter/hide treats for enrichment
  • For window barking: say "thank you", close curtains if needed
